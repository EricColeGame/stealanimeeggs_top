### Reading Spawns and Carry Mechanics

Eggs typically appear in waves rather than trickling in one at a time. Player experience suggests the first minute after a wave is the safest window to move, because most defenders are still busy grabbing their own eggs. Once the map settles, the risk of running into a defender rises sharply.

Carry mechanics matter just as much. In many versions of this genre, carrying an egg slows you down or blocks you from sprinting. If that is true in your game, plan a shorter route to your base rather than a longer, safer one — speed beats stealth when you are visibly loaded down.

## Base Defense: Protecting Your Anime Egg Pets

Defense in Steal Anime Eggs pets is not about building a fortress. It is about making your base annoying enough that a thief picks an easier target. Most raids are opportunistic, so friction — not invincibility — is the goal.

| Defense layer | What it does | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locks and gates | Slows entry to the main plot | Bypassed if you forget to re-arm them |
| Chokepoint layout | Forces thieves into a single visible path | Requires deliberate base design |
| Elevation | Puts pets out of easy reach | Some maps allow climbing |
| Decoy eggs | Wastes a thief's time on low-value targets | Only works once per player |
| Active presence | Simply being at your base | Does not help while you are offline |

Two habits matter more than any single upgrade. First, re-lock your base every time you leave it — an unlocked plot is the single most common reason players lose a legendary. Second, spread your best pets across more than one storage point if the game allows it, so one successful raid cannot wipe your entire collection.

## Trading, Value, and Long-Term Progression

Once your base is stable, trading becomes the real engine of progress. Hatch luck is random; trade value is not. Players who learn which pets are in demand can fill collection gaps without grinding the same egg pool for weeks.

| Situation | Recommended move | Why |

A simple rule keeps most players out of trouble: never trade a pet you cannot replace. If a companion is carrying your income, keep it until you have a duplicate or a clearly better option in hand.

No. You can build a strong collection through hatching and trading alone. That said, stealing is usually the fastest way to access high tiers early, which is why most experienced players mix both approaches.

Watch what other players are carrying and where they are heading. Bases that players return to repeatedly almost always hold something valuable. Community reports also suggest that freshly spawned eggs are more contested than older ones.

**Do rarity odds ever change?**
Yes. Limited-time pools, seasonal events, and balance patches regularly shift which tier is easiest to pull. Re-check the in-game egg list after each major update rather than relying on old tier lists.
