- **Learn the respawn timer of your own eggs.** You'll know exactly how long you have before the original owner notices.

Yes, but it's slow. Hatching with stacked luck boosts works; stealing from a base that already has one is simply faster and cheaper. Most players use a mix of both.

**Do luck boosts affect stolen eggs?**
No. Luck modifiers apply when an egg is generated, not when it changes hands. That's exactly why **Steal Anime Eggs egg rarity** makes theft so valuable — you inherit someone else's lucky roll for free.

**What's the fastest way to raise my collection value?**
Upgrade inventory capacity first, rebirth second, and chase rarity third. More slots mean more rolls, more rebirths mean better multipliers, and better multipliers mean the rare tiers finally show up.

**Should I display my rarest eggs?**
Only if you can defend them. A visible Mythical attracts raids. Many experienced players keep one showpiece out and store the rest off-shelf.
