## What Are Steal Anime Eggs Gamepasses?

Gamepasses are permanent Robux upgrades bought once and kept forever on your account. In steal-and-collect games like this one, they usually fall into a few broad families: movement boosts, inventory expansions, automation, protection, and cosmetic flexes.
