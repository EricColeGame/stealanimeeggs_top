## The Fastest Ways to Farm Steal Anime Eggs Coins

Speed comes from doing fewer things better, not from running around constantly. Here's what actually moves the needle.

- **Stay in one server.** In most versions of the game, your eggs only tick while you're present. Server hopping resets your rhythm and leaves your base unattended.

## Raid Smart, Not Often: Timing Your Steals

Timing matters more than aggression. A player who raids three times with good information will out-earn someone who raids ten times on instinct. Watch the server list, note who's moving, and strike when attention is elsewhere.

| Situation | What You Should Do |
|---|---|
| Server is packed with active players | Farm your own base; raids are contested and risky |
